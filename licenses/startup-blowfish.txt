Startup Blowfish primitive and mathematical data

The project-written JavaScript implementation is under MPL-2.0; see LICENSE.
The Blowfish algorithm was designed by Bruce Schneier. His original paper
places the algorithm in the public domain and permits use by anyone:
https://www.schneier.com/academic/archives/1994/09/description_of_a_new.html
The designer's official algorithm/source page also states free use:
https://www.schneier.com/academic/blowfish/

The mathematical pi initialization values are from the official constant data:
https://www.schneier.com/wp-content/uploads/2015/12/constants-2.txt
That file credits Mike Schaudies for arranging the pi values. The numeric data
is retained with that attribution; it was not copied from a game binary.

Published numerical known answers (credited to Eric Young) are from:
https://www.schneier.com/wp-content/uploads/2015/12/vectors-2.txt
They are factual cipher inputs/outputs; no surrounding article prose is copied.
New synthetic inputs and factual outputs in the test fixture are project
authored under MPL-2.0. Their independent reference used the designer's C:
https://www.schneier.com/wp-content/uploads/2015/12/bfsh-sch.zip
The reference was used only to produce local expected data. Its source is not
included or translated into this distribution, and normal tests do not compile
or require it. No Paul Kocher/SSLeay/OpenSSL implementation is included.

The startup-specific effective-key domain and LE32 resource carrier are a
separate compatibility contract. The original paper's normal maximum key
length does not replace that proved caller behavior. No SDK/startup consumer
is enabled by this primitive alone.
